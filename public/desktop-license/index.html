The short version

  • You get a license to use the Bluefern desktop app; you don't buy the software itself.
  • Editing features need a Bluefern account with a paid seat. Viewers can use the app read-only.
  • Please don't take the app apart, copy it or get around its seat check.
  • The app never contains Rockwell software. It uses the Logix Designer SDK from your own Studio 5000.
  • Always test a program on your own equipment, under your own safety procedures, before you run it.

This summary is here to help. The full text below is what applies.

1. This agreement

By installing or using the Software, you accept this agreement. If you install it for a company, you confirm that you are allowed to accept it for that company, and "you" means the company too.

Your use of Bluefern accounts and hosted repositories is covered by the Bluefern Terms of Service (bluefern.io/terms/). How we handle personal data is described in the Privacy Policy (bluefern.io/privacy/).

2. Licensed, not sold

We license the Software to you; we don't sell it. We keep all rights in it that this agreement doesn't give you.

While you follow this agreement, you may install and use the Software on Windows computers that you or your company control, for your own business. The license is personal to you: it is non-exclusive, and you can't transfer it.

3. Accounts and seats

You sign in to the Software with a Bluefern account. What you can do depends on your role:

  • Owners and members of an organization hold a paid seat and can use every feature, including staging, committing, pushing, pulling, merging, and converting or rebuilding Studio 5000 projects.
  • Viewers don't need a seat and can use the Software read-only: browse history, diffs and ladder views.

The Software checks your seat when it starts and from time to time after that. If it can't reach the server, it keeps working for up to 14 days after the last successful check.

The Software includes open-source components, such as Electron and Git. Each comes with its own license, which applies to that component and is listed with the Software. Nothing in this agreement limits your rights under those licenses.

5. Rockwell software

The Software contains no Rockwell Automation software, and we never ship or bundle it.

To convert Studio 5000 ACD files, the Software uses the Logix Designer SDK from your own Studio 5000 installation on your PC. Getting the right Rockwell licenses, and following their terms, is up to you. Without SDK access you can use Save As L5X in Studio 5000 instead.
