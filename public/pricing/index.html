Questions

About seats, payment and your code.

Who counts as an engineer?

Anyone who commits changes, opens or merges pull requests, or resolves merges. People who only view and comment don't need a seat.

Do our customers need seats?

No. Give a customer's maintenance team viewer access to a machine's repository and they see its history and every change, at no cost.

Do I still need Studio 5000 or TwinCAT?

Yes, to edit your projects. Bluefern doesn't replace your PLC software: engineers keep editing in it, and reviewing works in the browser.

Where is my code stored?

In a standard git repository on our servers in the United States, backed up every hour. You can clone all of it at any time.

Can we pay by invoice or purchase order?

Yes. Yearly plans ($450 per engineer) are invoiced, so purchasing can raise a purchase order. Monthly plans are paid by card.

Will the price go up?

Early-access teams keep $45 per engineer a month for 2 years from when they start paying.
