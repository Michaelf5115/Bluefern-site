The short version

  • We collect what we need to run the service: your account, the code you push, what you send us, and basic server logs.
  • We don't sell personal data, show ads, track you across other sites or use your code to train AI models.
  • Everything is stored in the United States, with Amazon Web Services, and backed up encrypted with Backblaze.
  • You can delete your account in the app or ask us to delete your data by email.

This summary is here to help. The full text below is what applies.

1. Who we are

Bluefern ("we", "us") runs git hosting for automation teams: the website bluefern.io, the web app at app.bluefern.io and the Bluefern desktop app. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who helps us process it and what you can ask us to do.

Questions or requests: hello@bluefern.io.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and password. We store the password only as a salted, deliberately slow hash, never in a form we can read. We also keep the organizations you belong to, your role and any invitations.
  • Repository contents: the code and files you push, and the pull requests, comments, approvals and merge resolutions you make. Commits carry the name and email address set in your git client.
  • Early-access requests: the name, work email, company, role, team size, platforms and notes you type into the early-access form.
  • Messages: what you write when you email us.
  • Server logs: IP address, date and time, the page or API address requested, and your browser's or git client's user agent. We use them to keep the service secure and to fix problems.
  • Billing data, once paid plans start: the company name, billing contact and invoices. Card payments will go through a payment processor, and we never see or store full card numbers.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising tags.

The web app sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It's needed for the app to work and isn't used for anything else.

The desktop app stores your sign-in on your own PC, encrypted by Windows. It talks to the server you sign in to and to the git hosts you add, checks for updates, and sends no usage analytics.

4. How we use it

  • to run the service: host your repositories, draw ladder views, compute diffs and merges, and keep backups;
  • to send the emails the service needs, such as invitations, password resets and notifications you've turned on;
  • to answer your questions and early-access requests;
  • to keep the service secure and find out what went wrong when something breaks;
  • to bill you, once paid plans start, and to meet legal obligations.

We don't sell or rent personal data, don't share it for advertising, and don't use your code or content to train AI models.

5. Who processes it for us

A few companies process data for us, only on our instructions and only to provide their part of the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (Amazon Lightsail, in the United States) runs our server, database and git repositories.
  • Backblaze (B2 cloud storage, in the United States) stores our backups. Backups are encrypted on our server before they're sent, so Backblaze can't read them.
  • Resend sends the service's emails, such as invitations and password resets. It receives the recipient's address and the message.
  • Cloudflare runs the DNS for our domain, hosts this website and forwards email sent to our addresses to us. It sees visitors' IP addresses and the messages it forwards.

We'll add any new processor, such as a card payment provider, to this list before it handles personal data. We may also disclose data if the law requires it, or to a company that takes over our business, which would then be bound by this policy.

6. Where it's stored

All data is stored in the United States. If you use the service from another country, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account data: while your account exists.
  • Repository contents: until you delete them, or 30 days after your organization's subscription or pilot ends.
  • Early-access requests: until early access ends, or sooner if you ask.
  • Server logs: only as long as we need them for security and troubleshooting.
  • Backups: taken every hour and kept as 24 hourly, 30 daily and 12 monthly copies. Something you delete is removed from the live service at once, and from the last encrypted backup within 12 months.

8. Your choices and requests

You can see and correct your account details in the web app, and clone your repositories at any time.

You can delete your account yourself on the Account page of the web app. If you're the only owner of an organization, first make someone else an owner or delete the organization.

You can also email hello@bluefern.io to ask for a copy of your personal data, to correct it, to delete it or to stop us using it. We'll answer within 30 days. If your account belongs to your employer's organization, we may ask the organization's owner to confirm requests about its repositories.

9. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, and sign-in links and access tokens only as fingerprints. Backups are encrypted before they leave our server. Access to the server is limited to the people who run it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we'll tell you without undue delay.

10. Children

Bluefern is a tool for businesses and isn't meant for children under 16. We don't knowingly collect their data.

11. California residents and Do Not Track

This section answers the questions California's Online Privacy Protection Act asks.

  • Effective date: this policy applies from the date at the top of this page.
  • Do Not Track: we don't track visitors across other websites, and our site has no third-party tracking, so it works the same way whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.
  • Third parties: we don't let other companies collect information about your activity over time and across websites through our site.
  • Reviewing and changing your information: see section 8.

12. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. For important changes, we'll also email account holders at least 30 days before they apply.

13. Contact

Bluefern, hello@bluefern.io.
